Requirements:
- I want to make a visualisation of the transportation methods that I have used recently.
- I will gather 100 pieces of data as to the methods of transport that I have used, including:
  the date, 
  the type,
  the time I touched in,
  whether the transport method was late or not,
  the time I touched out (if applicable as bus routes do not give a touch out time),
  the reason for why I travelled.
- The data will span around 30-45 days of travel dependent on if I go out on a specific day.
- The data I collect will use abstraction to remove unnecessary details in order to protect my identity, such as: 
  the route numbers, 
  my final destination,
  my beginning destination.
